A single one-line entry, written by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else.

## The line

> I didn't eat your homework. I filed it. In my stomach. It was overdue.

## Why this line

- **Dog logic, not guilt:** the dog does not confess or apologise — it reclassifies the crime as paperwork. Eating becomes filing, and the stomach becomes the filing cabinet.
- **Zero remorse, as the brief weighs:** the excuse improves the longer it goes. By the last beat the dog is not defending itself; it is citing a deadline the homework missed.
- **The punch is the last word:** "overdue" reframes the whole line — the dog was not hungry, it was enforcing policy.
- **None of the banned stock lines:** no cat, no taste of paper, no good boy. The mechanism is bureaucracy, which the brief's stock list never touches.
- **Tight:** 70 characters against the 200-character gate, in four short beats a person can retell verbatim.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts            # writes /output/entry.txt
OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
